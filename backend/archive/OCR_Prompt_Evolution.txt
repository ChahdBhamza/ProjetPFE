================================================================================
ARCHITECTURAL RATIONALE: VLM vs. CLASSIC OCR
================================================================================
The DetectionAppPFE pipeline utilizes a Multimodal Vision Language Model (VLM) 
rather than traditional OCR (e.g., Tesseract or Google Vision API) for several 
key academic and technical reasons:

1. SCENE UNDERSTANDING: 
   Unlike classic OCR which simply detects character shapes, the VLM understands 
   the geometry of an air conditioner. It can distinguish between the hardware 
   unit (secondary) and marketing banners (noise).

2. CONTEXTUAL FILTERING: 
   The VLM allows for "Semantic Logic" (e.g., "Ignore the digital temperature 
   display when extracting the model code"). This is computationally impossible 
   with pattern-based OCR.

3. DATA STRUCTURE: 
   The VLM performs "Classification" and "Extraction" simultaneously, outputting 
   RAG-ready JSON objects without requiring secondary NLP processing layers.

4. ROBUSTNESS: 
   The pipeline is optimized for non-ideal real-world photos (curved plastic, 
   low-contrast grey/gold logos, and variable camera distances).
